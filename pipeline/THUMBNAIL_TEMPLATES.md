# Thumbnail Templates (RULE 39)

Every video ships with **2 thumbnails**, 1280x720, ultra-realistic 4K HD style,
saved in the video folder as `thumbnail_1.jpg` / `thumbnail_2.jpg`.

## Type 1 — "Reaction split" (template ref: WILLIAM CROWNED)
- Layout: vertical split screen.
- **Left panel:** shocked / emotional reaction close-up of a key royal figure
  (e.g. hand over mouth, wide eyes).
- **Right panel:** the main event scene of the story (e.g. crowning moment).
- **Inset:** small portrait of the central subject in a red-bordered box,
  top-centre overlapping the split.
- **Banners:** red `BREAKING NEWS` banner, bottom-left.
- **Headline:** white bar across the bottom with a huge black ALL-CAPS quoted
  headline, e.g. `"WILLIAM CROWNED"`.
- Mood: dramatic, high-contrast, ultra-realistic.

## Type 2 — "Dual subject" (template ref: BIRTH RECORDS EXPOSED)
- Layout: diagonal white divider split screen.
- **Left panel:** subject A in a candid shocked moment (e.g. staring at phone).
- **Right panel:** subject B holding / reading documents (e.g. birth records).
- **Badges:** red `● LIVE` badge top-left; red kicker banner (`ROYAL UPDATE`)
  above the headline.
- **Headline:** white bar across the bottom with a huge black ALL-CAPS
  headline, e.g. `BIRTH RECORDS EXPOSED`.
- Mood: urgent, documentary, ultra-realistic.

## Build notes
- Compose from real pool photos of the actual subjects (never AI-generated
  faces of real people). Pick the most expressive / on-topic frame per panel.
- Text must be sharp at 1280x720 and readable at small YouTube preview sizes:
  headline ≥ 120px cap height, banners ≥ 60px.
- Keep faces unobstructed by text; headline bar overlaps the bottom ~22%.
