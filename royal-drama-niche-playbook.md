- Yellow border: 6-8px solid yellow (#FFD500 or similar bright yellow)

- Background: Pure white

## 📌 SECTION 8 — SCRIPT STRUCTURE STANDARD

### Length Options:
- **Short-form (Regalvora style):** 10-15 minutes / ~1,500-2,000 words
- **Medium-form (most channels):** 25-30 minutes / ~3,500-4,500 words
- **Long-form (Ritual Archive, Crown Watch style):** 40-45 minutes / ~5,500-6,500 words

### Universal Script Requirements:

**HOOK (First 30 seconds — mandatory elements):**
- Time-jump anchor ("29 years after...")
- Specific date + location + time ("at Balmoral, on Tuesday August 25, 2026, at 2:48 PM")
- Mysterious character intro (unnamed initially)
- Negation stack (3 denials that build curiosity)
- Villain discomfort teased (not explicitly named)

**MID-VIDEO REHOOKS (mandatory):**
- Rehook #1 at ~30% mark with unique comment phrase
- Rehook #2 at ~65% mark with different comment phrase
- Final CTA at end with third comment phrase
- Never reuse comment phrases within same video

**ICONIC QUOTE PLACEMENT:**
- 4-6 engineered iconic quotes per script
- Each under 15 words
- Poetic, timeless, screenshot-worthy
- Placed at end of major beats

**LANGUAGE REQUIREMENTS:**
- Present tense for scenes ("William asks", not "William asked")
- Qualifying language throughout ("the account claims", "reports suggest")
- Rule of 3s for rhythm (3 parallel constructions at emotional peaks)
- Sentence length variation (long/long/long/SHORT pattern)
- Minimum 15 specific numerical/date anchors per script

**FORBIDDEN LANGUAGE:**
- "delve into" / "let's explore" / "in conclusion"
- "in this video" / "today we're going to"
- "buckle up" / "without further ado"
- Excessive exclamation marks
- Modern slang ("literally", "actually", "vibe")

## 📌 SECTION 9 — POLICY SAFETY & ANTI-DETECTION

### YouTube Inauthentic Content Policy Compliance:

1. **Qualifying Language Required** — Never state fabricated events as fact. Use "reports suggest", "the account claims", "sources close to the situation say"

2. **Dramatization Framing** — At least once in Act 1, position story as reconstruction ("According to the account being described...")

3. **No Direct Defamation** — Never accuse living royals of specific crimes. Frame villain behavior through overreach, misjudgment, institutional pressure

4. **No Deepfake Implications** — Never write direct quotes attributed to real royals without qualifying frame

5. **AI Disclosure** — Include AI disclosure in channel About section per Palace Beacon / Ritual Archive templates

### Anti-Detection Rotation Rules:

1. Never open two consecutive scripts with same first three words
2. Never use same iconic quote twice across any videos
3. Never use same insider role twice in same rotation cycle
4. Never use same comment phrase twice
5. Publish at irregular times (3pm, 9pm, 11am alternating) — not daily fixed slot
6. Rotate between at least 5 different DNAs across 10 uploads
7. Never structure first 3 paragraphs identically to any previous script

### Sub-Niche Mixing Warnings:

- ⚠️ **NEVER mix Harry/Meghan content with Camilla/Diana content in same video** — audiences are different, engagement collapses
- ⚠️ **Diana's death circumstances = age-restriction risk** — keep dignified, avoid medical/graphic details
- ⚠️ **Financial claims about royals require "sources say" framing** — otherwise misinformation flag
- ⚠️ **Cross-celebrity content only when real event happens** — never fabricate celebrity deaths/events
